## Winner

> My name appeared in this morning's obituaries, and the photograph they used was taken an hour ago.

**98 characters, one sentence.** Why it wins:

- **Frightening (strongest criterion):** the threat is to the reader themself, and it is
  imminent — your death is already typeset, and someone photographed you an hour ago
  to prepare it. The dread outlasts the line: either you die today, or you are already
  dead and don't know it. Earned by implication, never announced.
- **Vivid:** concrete, specific detail — this morning's obituaries, the photograph,
  an hour ago. A reader can see it.
- **Original:** the obituary + fresh-photograph turn is not a mirror/doll/basement
  cliché and not a variant of any other candidate's angle.
- **Crafted:** tight, two-beat rhythm (public record → private photo), last words
  "an hour ago" land the blow.
